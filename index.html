About the Project

ASL Fingerspelling is a real-time American Sign Language recognition app that runs entirely in the browser. Point your camera at your hand, sign a letter, and it appears on screen — no installs, no plugins.

The system uses MediaPipe to detect 21 hand landmarks per frame, normalises them into a 72-feature vector, and sends them to a Random Forest classifier running on the backend. Predictions are smoothed over a 7-frame buffer and must remain stable before a letter is committed. J and Z use Dynamic Time Warping on wrist-path trajectories since they require motion.
