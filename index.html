About the project
Abhash Aryal
CS Student · Builder of this project

ASL Fingerspelling is a real-time American Sign Language recognition app that runs entirely in the browser. Point your camera at your hand, sign a letter, and it appears on screen — no installs, no plugins.

The system uses MediaPipe to detect 21 hand landmarks per frame, normalises them into a 72-feature vector, and sends them to a Random Forest classifier. Predictions are smoothed over a buffer and must hold stable before a letter commits.

Privacy & Data
Privacy & Data Use
What this app collects, what it doesn't, and your rights
Recognition — No data stored

When you use the sign recogniser, your camera feed never leaves your device. MediaPipe detects your hand landmarks entirely in your browser. The landmark coordinates are sent to the backend server only to run the classifier, and are not stored, logged, or retained in any form. This site uses Google Analytics to count visitors. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

Your video feed is never recorded or transmitted anywhere.

Contributions — What gets saved

When you voluntarily submit a training contribution, the following data is saved to a private Google Sheet: a timestamp, the letter label you selected, the 63 normalized hand landmark coordinates, and 9 joint angle values.

No images, no video, no names, no IP addresses, and no personally identifiable information are stored. The landmark data cannot be used to identify you.

Age requirement

The contribution feature collects biometric-derived data (hand landmarks) for the purpose of training an AI model. Under U.S. law (COPPA) and related regulations, you must be at least 13 years old to submit contributions without parental consent.

By submitting a contribution you confirm that you are 13 or older, or that you have obtained parental or guardian permission.

Limitations & risks

This is a student research project, not a commercial product. Data submitted via contributions is stored in a Google Sheet controlled by the project author and may be used to retrain the model.

Contributions are voluntary. You can stop at any time simply by not submitting. There is currently no automated way to request deletion of a submitted contribution — contact the project author directly via GitHub if you wish to have your data removed.
